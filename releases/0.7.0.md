# 0.7.0: Organisations, CV fit on their candidates' CVs

Cut from branch `release-0.7.0` on 2026-11-22. The Owner, on 2026-11-21, decided that ProteinCV serves Organisations too: companies that run CV fit on their candidates' CVs, who stay the CVs' Owners (ADR 0015). Switzerland first, every deal made by hand, and nothing kept. This release builds what a first Organisation needs for that.

## What was done

- **Organisations and their members.** An Admin creates an Organisation once its contract is signed, with its name, the contract's date and its order number, and adds members by name and work email. A member sets a password through an emailed link and signs in to the Organisation's pages. A member's Account is used for its Organisation only: no CVs, Protected CVs, Test fit, Past fits, Promo Codes or Subscription of its own.
- **One Balance per Organisation, in CHF.** An Admin credits it when an invoice is paid. Each CV fit run on one CV draws the Organisation price per run, USD 0.50 by default, always above the ordinary price per run, converted to CHF at the rate in use. There is no yearly fee.
- **CV fit through the API for a member**, with a Prote Key of their own and no Subscription, paid from the Organisation's Balance. A member's key opens CV fit only.
- **Batch fit.** One job description and up to 50 CVs give one Fit Report and Fit percentage per CV, in the order uploaded. The Balance must cover the whole Batch fit before anything is sent.
- **Keep nothing.** A Batch fit's CVs and reports are held in memory only and shown once: until the member leaves the results, or 10 minutes after the run ends, and while their email has not gone. The results go by email to the member who ran it, as one PDF of every Fit Report and one CSV.
- **A CV that carries a Notice is never scored.** It is not sent to the classifier, costs nothing, and is listed as its owner objecting, for a person to read.
- **A person decides.** No cut-off, no reject action, no sorting by Fit percentage, and every result for an Organisation says a person reviews the CV before any rejection.

## Known limits

- **Not ready for a first Organisation yet.** The contracts in `docs/legal/organisations/` are drafts for a lawyer's review, and their checklist is open: the database and files move to ProteinCV's own machines before a first Organisation runs anything.
- **Only "keep nothing".** Keeping Fit Reports or everything, the access log, and self-serve invite links are not built.
- **The email service keeps the results.** Resend keeps every sent email, attachments included, for 30 days in the United States.
- **A Batch fit is checked, not reserved.** The Balance and the hour's limit are checked for the whole Batch fit when it starts; API runs by other members meanwhile can use them up, and its later CVs are then listed as refused, costing nothing.
- **A restart loses a Batch fit under way**, with no email; the CVs it finished stay drawn.
- **Nobody has looked at the new pages in a browser**, opened the email in a mail client, or opened the CSV in a spreadsheet; they were checked through tests.
- **The contracts are not legal advice** and have not been reviewed by a lawyer.
- **The Admins' CV fit prices page still names the provider**: only Admins see it.
- The known limits of 0.6.7 stand.

## How to run

```bash
git checkout v0.7.0
corepack pnpm install
corepack pnpm -r build
corepack pnpm exec wrangler deploy
```

At the first start the new tables and columns are made, and every Account from before is an Owner's. Nothing else is needed: Organisations exist only once an Admin makes one on `/admin/organisations`.
