# 0.5.2: Terms and a 14-day withdrawal right

Cut from branch `release-0.5.2` on 2026-10-16. proteincv.com now has [Terms](https://proteincv.com/terms) that say who sells the Subscription and what you can expect when you buy one.

## What was done

### Terms

- **Terms** at [proteincv.com/terms](https://proteincv.com/terms), linked from the bottom of every page, with a **Contact** link to support@proteincv.com.
- **14 days to change your mind**: you can withdraw from a Subscription within 14 days of buying it, without giving a reason, and get the full amount back. Write to support@proteincv.com.
- **Cancel at any time**: the Subscription then runs until the end of the year you paid for and does not renew. After the 14 days, payments are not refunded.
- **Next to the Subscribe button**: a line says it renews every year until you cancel and that you have 14 days to withdraw.
- **Deleting your account does not cancel your Subscription**: write to support@proteincv.com to cancel it first. The delete form says so too.

## Known limits

- The Terms have not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.
- Cancelling and withdrawing are by email for now.
- Payments may still be in Stripe's test mode, until the Operator switches them to live.

## How to run

proteincv.com deploys with `corepack pnpm exec wrangler deploy`, as `DEPLOY.md` describes in "proteincv.com on Cloudflare". There is no database change and there are no new settings.

To update a running Docker deployment:

```bash
git fetch origin
git checkout v0.5.2
# back up the database volume first (DEPLOY.md, "Back up and restore")
docker compose up -d --build
```
