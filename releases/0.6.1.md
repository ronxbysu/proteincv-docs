# 0.6.1: the home page leads with CV fit, and a CV has limits on its length

Cut from branch `release-0.6.1` on 2026-11-15. A small release on top of 0.6.0: proteincv.com shows CV fit first, the list of Protected CVs fits beside its form, and files no CV looks like are refused, so the site never needs more than a quarter of its memory.

## What was done

- **The home page leads with CV fit** where it runs on Jev: the CV fit band sits right under the hero, the page's title, description and structured data name the CV fit check, the FAQ has three CV fit questions, and `llms.txt` describes it.
- **No "later release".** A server with neither an AI model nor a TypeSafe key leaves CV fit out of its home, sign-in and Privacy pages.
- **Your Protected CVs is a list of rows**, each with its format, what it carries, its Notice parts and its Document ID.
- **A CV has limits on its length.** A PDF of more than 10 pages is refused, and a PDF or Word document with more than 200,000 characters of text. A Word document that unpacks to more than a CV holds is refused too. Protect, Inspect and CV fit refuse them on the website, in the API and in the CLI; the Guard still reads any length.

## Known limits

- The Container is still the `basic` instance type. The limits were made to halve its memory, but Cloudflare refuses a custom size under 1 vCPU, so its cost is unchanged.
- The text limit is checked after a page's text is read, so one page of very small type is read once before it is refused.
- What keeps the Container awake nearly all month has not been looked into; that, more than its size, is what it costs.
- The known limits of 0.6.0 stand.
