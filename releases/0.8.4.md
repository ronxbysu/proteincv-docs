# 0.8.4: Skill names that are names

Cut from the `release-0.8.4` branch on 2026-11-27. Related experience lists the Skill names of a CV next to a requirement it does not plainly meet. The real end-to-end run of 0.8.2 showed it taking phrases that are no name: verbs a line of tasks starts with ("Develop", "Design"), plain words ("team", "production") and names with words after them ("MariaDB clusters"). Some were then read as core, or paired with an employer.

## What was done

- **Verbs and plain words are no Skill names.** The classifier is asked to say no to a verb for a task and to a common word standing alone. On the Fit Check Set's labelled phrases, 2 of 6 verbs still get through, where all 6 did, and none of 5 plain words, where 2 did.
- **A name with words after it is read as the name.** "MariaDB clusters" is read as "MariaDB", "MongoDB replica set" as "MongoDB". 1 of the set's 4 such phrases is still taken whole.
- **Names learnt before are checked again.** The list of Skill names ProteinCV has learnt keeps the version of the questions that confirmed each one. A name confirmed before 0.8.4 is asked again the next time a test finds it.

On the set, 151 of 178 expected Skill names are found (153 before) and 24 extra names are taken (31 before). Of the 20 labelled phrases that are no name, 4 are still taken (12 before).

## Known limits

- **A word on the line can go either way.** "Design" (0.76), "Planen" (0.50) and the employer "Pixelmill" (0.51) are still taken; 0.8.3 left Pixelmill out. Whether to read a yes from higher than 0.5 is left for a later release.
- **More questions per test.** A test asks a few more questions of Skill names, a fraction of a cent per test at ProteinCV's prices. A CV with very many multi-word phrases reaches the limit sooner, and its report says Related experience was not looked for.
- **A Past fit is made again.** Asking the same CV and job description again runs a new test, since the questions changed.
- The known limits of 0.8.2 stand.
