# 0.6.2: Test fit on the website is free and takes any CV

Cut from branch `free-test-fit` on 2026-11-15. On proteincv.com the Test fit page showed "CV fit has no price in CHF yet" in place of its form. Now nothing stands between a signed-in person and a test but a confirmed email.

## What was done

- **Test fit is free on the website.** No Subscription, no Prote Key, no Balance, and no price has to be set. Nothing is drawn. An Account whose email is not confirmed is asked to confirm it first.
- **Any CV, and the CV comes first.** The form leads with the CV: one of your kept Protected CVs, chosen from a list, or any CV uploaded, yours or someone else's. The job description comes below.
- **Your own Protected CV can be tested.** One chosen from the list is read without its Notice and Footer. If the Notice cannot be confirmed as taken out, nothing is sent and the original is asked for. A Protected CV that is uploaded is still refused.
- **The API is unchanged.** CV fit through the API is still paid from the Balance, with a Subscription and a Prote Key.

## Known limits

- Every free test costs the Operator at TypeSafe. The limit is 30 tests an hour per Account; nothing bounds the site as a whole but TypeSafe's prepaid credit.
- The new form was tested through its HTML, not looked at in a browser before the release.
- The two sentences added to the Terms (free testing may be limited or ended; a CV that is not yours only with its person's agreement) have not been reviewed by a lawyer.
- The known limits of 0.6.1 stand.
