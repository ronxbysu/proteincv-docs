# 0.8.1: Related experience

Cut from branch `release-0.8.1` on 2026-11-26. A Fit Report gave one verdict per requirement from what the CV says, so a CV that shows MySQL against a job that asks for PostgreSQL got "never did", and nothing said what in the CV came close. It now lists, under a requirement the CV does not plainly meet, the Skill names of the CV that the classifier reads as related to one the requirement asks for: Related experience, a hint to check that is never counted in the verdicts or the Fit percentage (spec #395, ADR 0017).

## What was done

- **Related experience on the Fit Report.** Under a requirement marked never did, cannot as asked or unclear, "Related in the CV:" lists up to 3 Skill names of the CV, the surest first, with no number beside them: "MySQL" under a requirement asking for PostgreSQL, or "MySQL (asked: PostgreSQL)" where the requirement asks for several. Never under a can. The top of the report counts how many requirements have something related and says that it is a hint, not counted in the Fit percentage; an Owner or a Guest reads "If you have done what is asked, say so in your CV.", a member of an Organisation "Ask the candidate about it before deciding."
- **Everywhere a Fit Report goes.** An Account's and a Guest's Past fit, a Batch fit's reports and its emailed PDF, the Fit Report PDF (a page of its own, "Related experience"), a Past fit's JSON download and the API's JSON answer (`related_names` per requirement, where the spec's story 14 said `related`, and `related_experience` for the report). The Persona's Fit Report on the Mac site has none.
- **Skill names, found by ProteinCV's own code.** It finds the names of skills, tools, technologies, methods, languages and certificates in the CV and in each open requirement: those it knows by matching, any other short item by asking the classifier once whether it is one. Then it asks, for each pair of a CV name and an asked name, whether the two are the same kind of technology, tool or work. Only short words and phrases are sent for this, never the CV's whole text. The classifier writes nothing.
- **Asked once, ever.** The names the classifier confirms join a list ProteinCV keeps, and its answer for every pair is kept, so a pair already answered is never sent again, for anyone, while the classifier and the pair question stay the same; a change to another question's wording keeps every answer.
- **A phrase joined by a small word must be one name.** Pieces of a line joined by a word such as "of", "on" or "von" are asked about whole as well as in their parts, so that "Ruby on Rails" can be found. Such a phrase now joins the list only when the classifier also says it is one name, rather than two things joined by the small word, or a thing and the work, place or people it was used for; on the measured set the first question alone said yes to 9 of 12 such phrases, among them "Spring Boot on PostgreSQL" and "Leitung von Wohnbauprojekten". The second question goes in the same request, so a run sends no more requests than before. Neither list holds any text a name was found in, nor whose test it was.
- **A person's name is kept out.** The code never asks about the Owner's name where it finds it, and the classifier is asked to say no to any name of a person.
- **When it is not looked for.** A CV and a job description that make too many pairs, or a look that fails, are said on the report, which keeps its verdicts and its Fit percentage; nothing related then does not mean none is.
- **An Admin's threshold.** How sure the classifier must be before a name is shown is set on the Admin pages, 0.5 by default, as measured.
- **"Nothing is kept" is said exactly.** The Privacy page, the Terms, the API page, the FAQ, and a Batch fit's pages and its email now say: "ProteinCV keeps the names of skills, tools and technologies it finds (such as MySQL), and whether two such names are related, shared by every test, never with the text they were found in or whose test it was." The Organisation documents name it as the one exception to "keep nothing". The Privacy page lists, among what a Past fit holds, the Skill names it shows as related.
- **The home page, its FAQ, the CV fit page and `llms.txt`** say what Related experience adds.
- **What a run can cost.** The questions are part of every run; the price per run is unchanged. The largest run, which a Balance must hold before a run paid by its tokens starts, is now 832,000 input tokens: USD 0.035 at the classifier's price, USD 0.35 at ProteinCV's default prices (it was 192,000: USD 0.0081 at the classifier's price, USD 0.081 at the default prices). A typical run is about 50,000 input tokens (USD 0.0021 at the classifier's price, USD 0.021 at the default prices), so it costs the price per run.
- **Every earlier Past fit says it was made before Related experience existed**, and asking the same CV and job description again runs a new test.

## Known limits

- **The threshold was measured on 50 labelled pairs written by an agent, and nobody has reviewed the labels**: 20 related and 30 unrelated pairs of names, at which 0.5 found 20 of 20 and showed 1 of 30. How often an unrelated pair passes in real reports is not measured.
- **Answers near the threshold vary between runs.** The lowest threshold that met the pass rule was 0.40 on 2026-11-25 and 0.50 on 2026-11-26, with the same questions.
- **A shared word can pass as related**: mechanical ventilation is shown as related to ventilation systems (0.94 in the measurement).
- **Extraction misses some names**: German compounds and inflections (Wohnbau, Bauleitung, Rohbau) and names inside longer phrases (intensive care, paid media). With an empty list of names it found 137 of the 162 a person expects (2026-11-26, after the one-name question); a missed name is never paired. A name that holds a small word (Ruby on Rails, Internet of Things, Bauen im Bestand) is asked about whole as well as in its parts, but not when a sentence runs into it ("Applied design of experiments") or it is joined by "and" (Research and Development); a one-letter language (R, C) is found only as an item of a list ("R, Python, C"), not in a sentence ("statistics in R"). Asking about these whole adds about one question in sixteen, and each such phrase is asked a second question, whether it is one name: it joins the list only when both answers are yes. Measured on 2026-11-26 (43 requests, 133,922 tokens: USD 0.0056 at the classifier's price, USD 0.056 at the default prices): of 20 joined phrases, the 4 that are one name (Ruby on Rails, Internet of Things, Design of Experiments, Bauen im Bestand) joined the list and the 16 others did not, among them "Spring Boot on PostgreSQL", "Terraform on AWS" and the 9 the first question alone said yes to; the ten lines added for it gave 16 of 16 expected names, R and C among them. Two of the four were kept near the threshold (Bauen im Bestand at 0.62, Ruby on Rails at 0.72), and answers vary between runs, so such a name may on another day not be found. Once a one-letter name (R, C) is on the list, it is found wherever it stands as an item of a list, whatever the list is about: with every expected name on the list, the nurse's CV gave a C. Should both answers be yes for a phrase that is two things, the names of the list inside it are still found, so the phrase adds a name and hides none.
- **The saving of keeping every pair's answer is not measured.** Without it, the measured set used about 1.6 times the tokens of the first design, which paired CV lines (USD 0.013 a CV at the default prices against USD 0.008).
- **Long CVs were not measured**: the set's five CVs are short.
- **The form's price is an estimate.** It counts by the list of names as it stands, so it can come out low when the list grows before the run, by a name that splits an item into more names. The largest run, which the Balance must hold, is the bound.
- **Other people's names in a CV** (a referee, a manager) are kept off the shared list only by the classifier's answer; 7 of 7 such names were answered no in the measurement. A name written surname first, or split over two PDF lines, is left to that answer too.
- **The kept names are not deleted with an account** or when an Organisation's contract ends: they are tied to neither.
- **A requirement that names no skill or tool has nothing to compare**, so it shows nothing related.
- **Nothing was checked by eye in a browser yet**: the Fit Report page, the PDF page and the changed public pages were checked by the tests only.
- The known limits of 0.8.0 stand.
