# 0.6.4: new Accounts and Guests for Admins, with a chart; the site and the API no longer say "Jev"

Cut from branch `release-0.6.4` on 2026-11-18. The site could not say how many people signed up last week; now its Admins' index can.

## What was done

- **Accounts and Guests on `/admin`.** A card for the days chosen: the last 7, 30 or 90 days, or any two dates. With none chosen it shows the last 7 days. It gives the totals of new Accounts, new Guests, Guests who made an Account and Accounts confirmed, a chart with a column a day, who is here now, and the same days as a table.
- **Counted on the day each was made.** A Guest stays counted after its 7 days and an Account after it is deleted. A Guest who signs up is a new Guest and a Guest who made an Account, not a second new Account.
- **The site and the API do not say "Jev".** They say "the classifier"; TypeSafe AI is still named where the page says where a test's text goes. The API's `ProteinCV-Model` header gives the version alone, `1.14.0` where it gave `jev-1.14.0`: a script that reads it must expect that. A Past fit kept before is shown with "the classifier" too.

## Known limits

- The days before this release are undercounted. The count starts from the Accounts and Guests still in the database; Guests that expired before then, and deleted Accounts, cannot be recovered, and a Guest who had already made an Account is counted as a new Account.
- The chart needs no script, so its tooltip is the browser's own and shows after a short pause.
- The known limits of 0.6.3 stand.

## How to run

```bash
git checkout v0.6.4
corepack pnpm install
corepack pnpm -r build
corepack pnpm exec wrangler deploy
```

One new table, `signups`, is made at the site's first start.
