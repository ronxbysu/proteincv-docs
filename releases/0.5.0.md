# 0.5.0: proteincv.com

Cut from branch `release-0.5.0` on 2026-10-15. The website is now public at [proteincv.com](https://proteincv.com): anyone can protect a CV there, the first one free, and keep up to 10 with a yearly Subscription.

## What was done

### The site on the internet

- **proteincv.com** runs on Cloudflare: the same website as the Docker deployment, without Claude Code, with its database on Turso, its Protected CV files in Cloudflare R2 and its emails sent through Resend. It sleeps when nobody uses it, so the first visit after a quiet spell takes a few seconds longer.
- **www.proteincv.com** sends you on to proteincv.com.
- It started empty: nothing was copied from the Operator's own site, which keeps running on the Mac.

### One free CV, then a Subscription

- **Your Free CV**: an account can protect one CV for free, keep it and download it. Deleting it does not give the place back, and neither does deleting the account.
- **A Subscription** keeps up to 10 Protected CVs for a year, paid through Stripe. When it ends you can still download every file you kept; you protect nothing new until you renew.
- **The price is in your currency, tax included**: Swiss francs in Switzerland and Liechtenstein, euros where the euro is used, US dollars everywhere else. Stripe charges what the site shows.
- The protect form says how many places you have left.

### What changed for everyone

- **Uploads are limited to 10 MB** (20 MB before), and a larger file is refused before it is read.
- **A Guest's file is not kept**: protect one CV without an account, download it straight away, and save it. Only a record that it was made stays.
- **Deleting your account** deletes everything it held except your email, kept marked deleted, so the Free CV cannot be taken twice. Signing up again with that email starts a new account.

### New pages

- **Inspect a PDF** (`/inspect`): upload any PDF and see whether it carries a ProteinCV Notice, which statements, on how many pages, and whether pages were changed afterwards. Nothing is kept.
- **Evidence** (`/evidence`): how Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Gemini read a Protected CV in our tests, with the screenshots and every run.
- **What AI does with your CV, and how to object** (`/guide/ai-screening`): a guide to automated screening and your rights in the EU and the UK. It is not legal advice.
- The pages now lead with protecting your CV, with real screenshots. Where no AI model is set up, as on proteincv.com, **CV fit** (the skills Profile, the Persona and the Fit Report) is announced for a later release.
- The site is easier to find and share: search engines get a sitemap and page descriptions, and links show a preview picture.
- The footer names the release the site runs.

### For the Operator

- **Admin pages**: set up Stripe and the prices, and keep the Free storage list, the emails whose accounts keep CVs with no Subscription and no limit. Each Admin email with no account gets one when the site starts, from a password hash the Operator sets.
- **A storage guard** stops new files before the file store's free tier runs out, and the admin page shows how much is used.
- A PDF that is only a header, with no document behind it, is now refused as not a PDF instead of failing.

## Known limits

- Payments may still be in Stripe's test mode, until the Operator switches them to live.
- The Cloudflare site with its Container was not tried locally with `wrangler dev`.
- After signing up you land on your Profile page, where the "Confirm your email" card is; after signing in, on Protected CVs.
- The guide's legal wording, the GDPR objection and the Rights line still await a lawyer's review.

## How to run

See `DEPLOY.md` in the repository, section "proteincv.com on Cloudflare", for the public site: it starts on a fresh database, and a new release deploys with `corepack pnpm exec wrangler deploy`.

To update a running Docker deployment:

```bash
git fetch origin
git checkout v0.5.0
# back up the database volume first (DEPLOY.md, "Back up and restore")
docker compose up -d --build
```

The new settings are all optional, and the Mac site runs as before without them. The first start adds the new columns and tables to the database on its own.
