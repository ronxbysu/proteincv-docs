# 0.6.3: one burger menu, and the pages are called Documents and Fit Check

Cut from branch `release-0.6.3` on 2026-11-15. A change to the website's header and to the names of two pages.

## What was done

- **One burger menu, at every width.** The header is the logo and a burger button; the row of links beside the logo is gone on wide screens too. Signed out, Get started and Sign in stand beside the burger on a wide screen and are in its panel on a narrow one.
- **Documents, Profile, Fit Check.** That is the signed-in menu. "Protected CVs" is now "Documents" and "Test fit" is "Fit Check": in the menu, as the heading of those two pages, and in every link that names them. Their addresses are unchanged. A Protected CV is still called that, and the button that runs a test still says Test fit.
- **The menu closes by itself** on Escape, on a click outside it and on a link chosen in it. With JavaScript off it still opens and works.
- **Test runs left the menu.** On a server with its own AI model it is linked from the Fit Check page.

## Known limits

- The signed-in menu with Fit Check in it, and the header at phone width, were covered by tests of the page's HTML but not looked at in a browser before the release.
- The known limits of 0.6.2 stand.

git checkout v0.6.3
corepack pnpm install

corepack pnpm exec wrangler deploy
