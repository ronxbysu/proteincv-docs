# 0.6.7: the home page leads with Test fit, and Test fit is paid for past one free per email

Cut from branch `release-0.6.7` on 2026-11-20. The Owner, on 2026-11-19: "the main feature now is Test Fit. So bring it up on the landing page. Then should be Protection feature. Update SEO best way you can to draw traffic to use "Test Fit" feature". On proteincv.com, where CV fit runs on the classifier, the home page's title already led with CV fit, but its hero was still Protection, and every Test fit was free to every confirmed Account, so its cost to the Operator had no bound. Now the hero is Test fit, Protection comes second, people who search to check a CV against a job description land on a page made for that question, and each Test fit past one free per email is paid for (ADR 0014).

## What was done

- **The home page leads with Test fit.** "Check your CV against a job description: one Test fit free per email", with the example Fit Report beside it, on a phone too, and two buttons: **Test my fit** (sign-up signed out, Fit Check signed in) and **Add a Notice for AI** (to Protection). Under the buttons, what is needed and what it costs. Then what a Test fit tells you, with its three steps; then Protection, with what the hero said before; then the real chats, what a Protected CV carries, how to protect one, the Guest form, the limits, the FAQ with the Test fit questions first, and a closing call that leads with Test fit.
- **A public page for CV fit, `/cv-job-match`**: how it works in three steps, the example Fit Report, what the verdicts mean, what it does not do (a classifier, not a person; not an ATS score; it reads what the CV says), where the CV's text goes, a FAQ, and the call to Test fit. The path names what people search for, a CV job match, in three short words that read well in a link. The signed-in Fit Check page stays at `/fit`.
- **Search.** Titles under 60 characters and descriptions under 160 that lead with the search phrase and say "CV or resume" and "One Test fit free per email" ("one free test per email" in the titles, too short for the whole phrase); the Open Graph and Twitter tags follow them, with the one shared image. The new page carries `WebPage`, `WebApplication` with Test fit's Offers, `FAQPage` and `BreadcrumbList`, with a visible breadcrumb; no rating, review or count anywhere. It is second in the sitemap and first among the pages in `llms.txt`, which now puts Test fit first and Protection second.
- **Links**: the signed-out menu ("CV fit" first), the footer, the home hero and FAQ, the sign-in and sign-up pages, and the Inspect, guide and Evidence pages link the new page, once each.
- **One Test fit free per email, then paid for.** A confirmed Account's first Test fit that makes a Fit Report is its Free Test fit, once per email, ever: deleting the Account and signing up again gives none, and an Account that ran a Test fit before 0.6.7 has had it. After it, a Promo Code for Test fit the Account holds pays for a Test fit on an uploaded CV; otherwise the Test fit is drawn from the Account's Balance, which any confirmed Account can now top up, with or without a Subscription. Asking again, a refused run and a run that fails at the classifier take nothing. An Admin's Test fits draw nothing.
- **The price.** Without a Subscription a Test fit costs what an API call costs: the price per run, USD 0.10 by default, or its input tokens when they come to more, which at the default prices they never do. With an active Subscription it costs the Subscriber price per run, USD 0.05 by default, whatever its tokens. Every price is set once in US dollars on the Admins' new CV fit prices page and shown in CHF or EUR through the European Central Bank's daily rate, or one an Admin types in, rounded up to the cent. The API's prices follow the same settings.
- **Before and after a run.** The Fit Check form says what the next Test fit costs before it runs: the Free Test fit, the Promo Code that pays with its uses left, the Subscriber price per run, or "This test costs" the price per run. The Past fit a Test fit lands on says what was drawn and the Balance after it, and the account page lists the latest Test fits and API calls with what each drew.
- **The pages say the prices.** The home page, `/cv-job-match`, the FAQ, `llms.txt` and the structured data say "One Test fit free per email; then $0.10 a run, or $0.05 with a Subscription", in the visitor's currency, read from the settings on every request, so a changed price cannot leave a page untrue.
- **The Terms, Privacy, the Balance card and the Profile page say what a Test fit costs** as ADR 0014 decides, and Privacy says the server downloads the ECB's public rates about once a day, sending nothing about anyone.
- **Every other site** (with its own model, or with neither) keeps its Protection-first home page, unchanged, and answers `/cv-job-match` with a 404: a redirect home would be a soft 404 to a crawler and lead to a page with no Test fit. A site with Test fit but no Stripe settings sells no top-up, so there a Test fit past the free one is "not for sale on this server".
- Every changed page was looked at in a browser at 1280 and 390 pixels wide: the Fit Check form in each of its cost states, a paid Past fit, the account page's Balance card, the Admins' CV fit prices page, the home page and `/cv-job-match` from Switzerland.

## Known limits

- **The form as first shown quotes the price per run, not this test's estimate.** The site has no client script, so the cost worked out from this CV and this job description shows only when the form comes back with them: after a refusal, or with "Run again anyway". At the default prices every run costs the price per run, so the quote is exact; at other prices "This test costs" can be less than the draw, which never exceeds the "at most" shown when there is one. The Owner kept it so (2026-11-21).
- **An email deleted before 0.6.7 can get a Free Test fit again.** Deleting an Account then deleted the records that would show it had one.
- **A server that stops mid-run holds part of a Balance, or the Free Test fit, for an hour.** A run's usage record holds the most it may draw before it runs; one left unsettled stops counting after an hour.
- **CHF and EUR round up to the cent.** At an ECB rate near 0.79, USD 0.10 is CHF 0.08 and USD 0.05 is CHF 0.04.
- **Search results take time.** A new page is crawled and ranked over weeks; nothing here can be measured until then. Google shows FAQ rich results only for a few kinds of site, so the `FAQPage` data is unlikely to show as one.
- **Someone signed out with no Promo Code still has to sign up and confirm an email** before a Test fit. A first test without an account is a decision for the Owner, not built.
- The known limits of 0.6.6 stand.

## How to run

```bash
git checkout v0.6.7
corepack pnpm install
corepack pnpm -r build
corepack pnpm exec wrangler deploy
```

Before deploying, keep at least one top-up pack in every currency on `/admin/subscriptions`; every price has a default, and the exchange rates come from the ECB at the first start. At the first start two new tables are made, `cv_fit_prices` (with its `set_at` columns) and `exchange_rates`, and three new columns, `accounts.free_test_fit_at` (every Account that ran a Test fit before is marked as having had its Free Test fit), `api_calls.test_fit_paid_by` and `api_calls.held_microcents`. The old `token_prices` table is left in place and no longer read. The server makes about one outbound request a day, to `www.ecb.europa.eu`. After deploying, open `/admin/cv-fit-prices` and check that CHF and EUR show the ECB as their source, with its date.
