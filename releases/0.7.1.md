# 0.7.1: Admin pages for Accounts, Organisations and Invoices

Cut from branch `release-0.7.1` on 2026-11-22, as a fix to 0.7.0. The Owner expected the Admin side of Organisations to look like a CRM: Organisations and Accounts in tables, a page for each, usage and Invoices. 0.7.1 gives the Admin pages that look and has them operate Subscriptions and Balances; a CRM's own records, contacts, notes and deal status, come in a later release (spec #360). The same day the Owner decided that Organisations are onboarded on Turso and R2, without waiting for ProteinCV's own machines (ADR 0015's note of 2026-11-22).

## What was done

- **An Admin menu** on every Admin page: Overview, Organisations, Accounts, Invoices, Settings.
- **Overview** adds a month's money: Subscriptions sold, top-ups, Organisation credits, CV fit runs and what they cost the Operator, the Balances held, and the Invoices owed.
- **Accounts**: every Owner's Account and member in a table, with each Owner's **Account use** (job seeker, recruiter, both, none yet), searched, sorted, filtered and downloaded as CSV; a page per Account with its facts, never a CV's content, a Posting or a Fit Report.
- **Changes with a reason**: an Admin grants, extends or ends a granted Subscription, and grants or corrects a Balance, never below zero. Each change is kept with who made it and when. A Subscription paid through Stripe stays in Stripe.
- **Organisations** in a table, and a page per Organisation with its members, billing details, Balance, corrections, and runs and draws by month and by member.
- **Invoices** (ADR 0016): made by the site for an Organisation, prepaid, in CHF, with no VAT, as a PDF with a Swiss QR-bill; sent by email or downloaded; marking one paid credits the Balance in the same write.

## Known limits

- **Nobody has looked at the new pages in a browser**; they were checked through tests.
- **The Invoice's PDF has not been seen, printed or scanned with a banking app.** Its text was checked in order and its QR code decoded in a test, from the drawing of the code rather than the finished PDF. Make a test Invoice and scan it before the first real one.
- **The QR-bill leaves the debtor out**: billing addresses are free lines and the QR-bill takes structured ones, so the "Payable by" box is empty and the billing name and address are printed on the invoice above it.
- **Payments are matched by hand**: an Admin marks an Invoice paid on seeing the money in the bank.
- **Not a CRM yet**: no contacts, notes, deal status or renewal dates.
- **Turso is a weak row in Annex 3** (`docs/legal/organisations/README.md`), and the contracts still await a lawyer's review.
- The known limits of 0.7.0 stand, but for the move to own machines, which no longer comes first.

## How to run

```bash
git checkout v0.7.1
corepack pnpm install
corepack pnpm -r build
corepack pnpm exec wrangler deploy
```

At the first start the two Balance ledgers are rebuilt once, every entry kept, and the new tables and columns are made. Then enter the IBAN at `/admin/iban` before making an Invoice.
