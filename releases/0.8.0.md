# 0.8.0: a console look, and Invite

Cut from branch `release-0.8.0` on 2026-11-24. The website had one burger menu at every width, tinted surfaces and rounded buttons. It now looks and works like a console: the menu is always there at the left of a signed-in page, the account's own matters are in the bottom left corner, and the look is plainer. Inviting also got pages of its own, for an Account and for a member of an Organisation (specs #386 and #393).

## What was done

- **A sidebar on signed-in pages.** An Account, a member, an Admin and a Guest with a Past fit have the menu at the left, with an icon per entry. Below 768px it is a drawer that a burger in a slim top bar opens.
- **The Account menu.** The sidebar's bottom left corner says who is signed in. Clicking it opens a menu upward: Account and Sign out for an Owner; its email, Account and Sign out for a member; Save your account and Sign in for a Guest with a Past fit. An Account or an Organisation with a Balance sees it in a card above.
- **A top bar for someone signed out.** The links, Sign in and Get started stand in the bar on a wide screen, and behind a burger on a narrow one.
- **The look.** Inter, a white page with hairline borders, square buttons in the site's cyan, an arrow on a button that opens another page. The tinted protein-ribbon surfaces of 0.3.0 are gone; the logo and the lilac of the Notice stay.
- **No examples or evidence on the landing page, for now.** The "In real chats" screenshots and the links to the Evidence page are switched off, to come back soon; the Evidence page is still at its address.
- **No landing page for someone signed in.** The home address sends an Account to My CVs and a member to its Organisation's home. An Account's footer no longer links the landing page's Limits, which it cannot open.
- **`llms.txt` names the real chats and the Evidence page only where they are shown.**
- **The Admins' Overview fits a phone and the space beside the sidebar**: its tiles go two to a row and its date fields share the width.
- **Still no JavaScript needed.** The drawer and the signed-out panel are popovers and the Account menu is `<details>`; the site's script only closes them.
- **Invite, for an Account.** A new entry in the menu, after Profile. The page makes a Promo Code two ways: "Generate promo code" shows its link once, to share yourself; "Email promo code" emails the link to an address you give. Your codes, and Revoke, are under them.
- **Invite members, for a member of an Organisation.** First in the member's Account menu. The member gives a colleague's name and work email; the colleague is added to the same Organisation and emailed a link to set a password.
- **The account page** keeps "Have a Promo Code?" and one line that sends to Invite.

## Known limits

- **The signed-out links stand in the bar from 1024px up, not 768px**: six links, the logo and two buttons do not fit below that.
- **In a browser without popovers** (before Chrome 114, Safari 17, Firefox 125) the sidebar, and the signed-out links, stand above the content on a narrow screen instead of opening as a drawer.
- **The Balance in the sidebar is read when the request starts**, so a page drawn by the same request that drew from the Balance shows the amount before the draw until the next page.
- **Pages were not laid out anew.** Only the shell and the design tokens changed; content laid out for a full-width page now has about 216px less on a wide screen.
- **Cyan is not only on buttons, links and focus.** It was taken off the page's surfaces, but it is still the accent of many small marks: the initials, the confirmation message, the Fit percentage row, the numbered cards and step numerals, the file-format tag, the closing call, the eyebrow lines, the "can" chips, the tick bullets, the FAQ's "+", the screenshot labels, a checked checkbox and the "CV" of the logo. The segmented control, the burger and the round tooltip button keep rounded corners; only the buttons proper were squared.
- **A member's Balance card links to its Organisation's home, where the Balance is**, not to its account page, and shows at zero too.
- **A Guest still gets the landing page**, where its one free protect is; only an Account is sent to My CVs.
- **The hero still says what Claude, ChatGPT and Grok did in our tests**, with no link to the evidence while it is hidden.
- **Checked by eye in headless Chrome only**, on a local preview (not the Docker one), at 1440px, 768px and 375px, for an Account, an Admin, a member and someone signed out. A Guest with a Past fit was looked at on its pages as the tests render them, with the site's styles, at 1280px and 375px, since the preview has no human check to run its free Test fit. Safari and Firefox were not looked at.
- **Nothing limits how many colleagues a member invites**, or how often, beyond the checks on each (a name, an email no Account has). Each invite sends one email from the site.
- **"Email promo code" sends mail to an address a subscriber types.** It is held only by the most live codes an Account may hold (5 by default); an Admin has no such limit.
- **A member cannot send the link again or remove a colleague**: only an Admin can. Where the email could not be sent, the page says to ask ProteinCV.
- **The email does not say who sent it.** It is the same email an Admin's send makes.
- **On a site that sells no Subscriptions**, Invite is in the menu and says that Promo Codes are not made there, unless the Account is an Admin.
- The known limits of 0.7.4 stand.
