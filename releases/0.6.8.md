# 0.6.8: the classifier's provider is named by its kind and country

Cut from branch `release-0.6.8` on 2026-11-21. The Owner, on 2026-11-21, decided that ProteinCV will serve Organisations, which run CV fit on their candidates' CVs (ADR 0015), and that the company running the classifier is named to an Organisation in its contract, not in public. So the pages that said where a CV's text goes named a company where the law asks only for its kind and country, and the Privacy page did not say what protects a transfer to the United States, which the law does ask for.

## What was done

- **The site says "a classifier provider in the United States"** where it named the company: on the home page, the Privacy page, the Test fit form, CV fit's public page and its FAQ. The API documentation says "the classifier".
- **The Privacy page says what protects the transfer**: "The transfer is protected by standard contractual clauses; write to support@proteincv.com for a copy of them, or for the provider's name." The name is given to anyone who asks.
- **Organisations are decided, not built** (ADR 0015): an Organisation's members share one Balance and pay an Organisation price per run; they send CVs one at a time through the API or up to 50 at once in a Batch fit; a CV that carries a Notice is never scored; a Fit Report supports a person's decision and never rejects anyone; each Organisation chooses whether nothing, its Fit Reports or everything is kept; Switzerland first.
- **Draft contracts for Organisations**, in `docs/legal/organisations/`: Terms, a processing contract with its list of subprocessors as a confidential annex, an order form, a notice for candidates and a record of processing. They are drafts for a lawyer's review.

## Known limits

- **Nothing for Organisations exists on the site.** No page, route or table: the contracts describe features still to build, and their checklist says what must be true before a first Organisation signs.
- **The contracts are not legal advice** and have not been reviewed by a lawyer.
- **The Admins' CV fit prices page still names the provider**: only Admins see it.
- The known limits of 0.6.7 stand.
